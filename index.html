Memorabília
Memorabilia

Memorabília is an ongoing collection of objects gathered from my own history, memory and obsessions.

Through rudimentary processes, I decontextualise these objects and transform them into images, creating an archaeological archive of fragments and traces.

Nos bons lugares há sempre vento
In good places there is always wind

Cíntia Ferreira (Lisbon, 1999) is a multidisciplinary artist working across photography, sculpture, film, performance and other forms of image-making.

Her practice begins with a fascination for the traces left by life, death, time and the landscape.
